# The role

Sources: the recruiter's role brief and prep pages, my round-1 prep notes, and what Kevin said in round 1. Confidential.

## The seat
- Senior hands-on IC: own a product surface end to end, customer problem → shipped, revenue-generating product. Pod of 1 PM + 1 engineer, shipping monthly
- Forward-deployed: with customers, proving value in weeks
- Builder: take my own work to a PR; engineers own the infrastructure
- Broad remit; sets how the team uses AI; brings enterprise stakeholders through change
- Must-haves: startup building experience; zero-to-one → revenue, with evidence; wants to stay IC
- London, 3 days a week; base to £150k+, plus options

## Two seats
- **Seat 1, Customer & UX / Discovery:** lives in the customer's problem; strong UX; work to a mergeable PR
- **Seat 2, Agentic AI:** model-driven products in production; evals in depth
- Both: zero-to-one into production and revenue

## Process
1. 45 min with Kevin Luo (VP Product) ✅
2. Live build with Kevin (see `live-session.md`)
3. Kamil Kluza (co-founder), if the build goes well
4. Lukky Ahmed (CEO)

## Round 1: what I learned
- I'm in for **Seat 1**; LLM fluency still expected
- Very discovery-centric; he asked about UI/UX and discovery as well as my agent and eval work
- PM directs the science team from discovery. E.g. hail: when do customers start to care? why? does it differ by customer?
- ROI = time saved + consultants avoided + better decisions
- Lead with commercial use cases (lending, investment), not safety

## My evidence
| They want | Me |
|---|---|
| Startup builder | Savvy (co-founded): customer problem → agent prototype → built and tested myself |
| Zero-to-one → revenue | Savvy: picked agent development over upskilling → 12 contracts, $252k; expansion, faster closes |
| Discovery and UX | Multiverse: ~25 interviews → 3 fixes; retention 87% → 97%, tenure 4.9 → 9.3 months, ~$9.5m |
| Trustworthy AI | ExaCare: 99.7% precision, 79% recall; 1,000-case golden set; shadow mode; kill switch |
| AI vs code | ExaCare: fixed logic deterministic, AI only where judgement is needed |
| Work to a PR | Design prompt → prototype → spec → one PR per screen, tests, feature flag |
| Regulated decisions | ExaCare (healthcare admissions) ≈ lending decisions |

## Positioning
- Lead with discovery, product judgement and UX: from what the customer says to the real workflow to a product they'd trust and use
- Evals and the AI/code split as supporting proof, not the headline

## Gaps, and my answer
- No climate or finance background → I'd have focused Savvy on finance in hindsight; the ramp-up in this repo
- "Why Climate X" led with saving lives → reframe: better lending and investment decisions
- Savvy revenue is small → Multiverse's ~$9.5m
