# The role

Sources: the recruiter's role brief and prep pages, my round-1 prep notes, what Kevin said in round 1. Confidential: keep this repo private. Recruiter feedback lives in my private Notion (ClimateX → Second-stage prep notes), not here.

## The seat
- Senior hands-on IC: own a product surface end to end, customer problem → shipped, revenue-generating product. Pod of 1 PM + 1 engineer, monthly releases
- Forward-deployed; builds to a PR; engineers own the infrastructure; sets how the team uses AI
- Must-haves: startup building, zero-to-one → revenue with evidence, wants to stay IC
- Two seats: **Customer & UX / Discovery** (mine) and **Agentic AI**
- London, 3 days a week; base to £150k+, plus options

## Process
1. 45 min with Kevin Luo (VP Product) ✅
2. Live build with Kevin (below)
3. Kamil Kluza (co-founder), if the build goes well: commercial impact, strategy
4. Lukky Ahmed (CEO)

## Live build
- Kevin sends a real Climate X data pack shortly before; I build a new feature live, screen shared
- He plays an enterprise customer → discovery first, past the ask to the real workflow; keep asking while building so every trade-off traces to something he said
- Two-way: he'll nudge; narrate as I go. Assessed on thinking and outcome, not polish
- What lands: narrate choices · get to a PR, not a spec · numbers in code, AI for interpretation, human checks, audit trail · keep the commercial thread (value, adoption, conservative buyers)

## Round 1: what I learned
- In for Seat 1; LLM fluency still expected
- Discovery-centric; he asked about UI/UX and discovery as well as agents and evals
- PM directs the science team from discovery. E.g. hail: when do customers start to care, why, does it differ by customer?
- ROI = time saved + consultants avoided + better decisions
- Lead with commercial use cases (lending, investment), not safety

## Positioning
- Lead with discovery, product judgement and UX: what the customer says → the real workflow → a product they'd trust and use
- Evals and the AI/code split as proof, not the headline

## Gaps, and my answer
- No climate or finance background → in hindsight I'd have focused Savvy on finance; the ramp-up in this repo
- "Why Climate X" led with saving lives → better lending and investment decisions
- Savvy revenue small → Multiverse's ~$9.5m
