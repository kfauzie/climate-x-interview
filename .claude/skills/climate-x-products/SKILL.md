# Climate X products

One skill, one file per product. Read only the file the task needs.

| Product | Question it answers | Main buyers | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spectra** | How much could *my* assets lose to physical climate risk? | Banks (credit and climate risk), real estate, asset managers, partners | `spectra.md` |
| **Adapt** | What should I do about it, and is it worth the money? | Real-estate owners and operators, facilities partners, banks (SME lending) | `adapt.md` |
| **Carta** | How exposed are companies I lend to, invest in or might buy? | Banks (corporate lending), asset managers, PE | `carta.md` |

## How they fit together [analysis]
- **Spectra → Adapt:** from risk to action. A high-loss asset becomes a costed adaptation case
- **Carta → Spectra:** from company to assets. Carta finds the sites, and Spectra's engine prices their risk
- Together they cover a customer's **own** assets (Spectra, Adapt) and **other companies'** (Carta), from measuring risk to acting on it

## Ground rules when using this skill
- Every point is tagged **[public]** (Climate X marketing, unverified), **[prep]** (interview preparation notes) or **[analysis]** (my inference)
- Present [analysis] items, especially opportunities, as hypotheses to test with the customer, never as facts
- Frame value commercially: time saved, consultant cost avoided, better lending and investment decisions
- Company and competitor background with sources: the `climate-x-company` skill
