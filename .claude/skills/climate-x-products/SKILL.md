# Climate X products

One skill, one file per product. Read only the file the task needs. Facts come from climate-x.com (read 2026-11-01) unless tagged otherwise.

| Product | Question it answers | Main buyers | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spectra** | How much could *my* assets lose to physical climate risk, under which scenario and when? | Banks (climate and credit risk), real estate, insurers, asset managers, partners | `spectra.md` |
| **Adapt** (and **Adaptation Finance**) | What should I do about it, what does it cost, and what's the ROI? Can a bank finance it? | Real-estate owners, facilities partners, banks (SME and corporate lending) | `adapt.md` |
| **Carta** | Where are the assets of companies I lend to, invest in or might buy? | Banks (corporate lending), investment banks, asset managers, PE | `carta.md` |
| **Infrastructure Screener** | What's the risk to a solar farm, pipeline or network that spans more than a point? | Infrastructure investors, utilities, energy developers | `infrastructure-screener.md` |

Access routes across products: web app, CSV upload and export, PDF reports, Enterprise API; Carta as a managed service.

## How they fit together [analysis]
- **Carta → Spectra:** from company to assets. Carta finds the sites; Spectra prices their risk
- **Spectra → Adapt:** from risk to action. A high-loss asset becomes a costed adaptation case
- **Adapt → Adaptation Finance:** from action to bank revenue. Resilience projects become loans
- **Screener:** the same engine for infrastructure geometry, with the traceable AI report pattern
- Together they cover a customer's **own** assets and **other companies'**, from measuring risk to acting on it and financing it

## Ground rules when using this skill
- Every point is tagged **[site]** (climate-x.com, Climate X's own claims), **[web]** (press or third parties), **[prep]** (interview preparation notes) or **[analysis]** (my inference)
- Present [analysis] items, especially opportunities, as hypotheses to test with the customer, never as facts
- Frame value commercially: time saved, consultant cost avoided, better lending and investment decisions, new revenue
- Company, regulation (PRA SS5/25), thought leadership and competitors, with sources: the `climate-x-company` skill
