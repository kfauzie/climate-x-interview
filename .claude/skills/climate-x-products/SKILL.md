# Climate X products

One file per product; read only the one needed. Facts from climate-x.com (Nov 2026); "my take" = my inference, to test with customers.

| Product | Answers | Buyers | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spectra | How much could my assets lose, under which scenario, when? | Banks, real estate, insurers, asset managers | `spectra.md` |
| Adapt | What should I do about it, what does it cost, what's the ROI? | Real-estate owners, facilities partners, banks | `adapt.md` |
| Carta | Where are the assets of companies I lend to or invest in? | Banks, investment banks, PE, asset managers | `carta.md` |
| Infrastructure Screener | What's the risk to a site that spans more than a point? | Infrastructure investors, utilities, developers | `infrastructure-screener.md` |

## How they connect
Carta (company → assets) → Spectra (assets → loss) → Adapt (loss → action, ROI) → Adaptation Finance (action → bank revenue). Screener = Spectra for infrastructure geometry.

Company, regulation and competitors: `climate-x-company`.
