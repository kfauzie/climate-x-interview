# Infrastructure Screener

## What it is
- For assets that don't fit on a point: solar and wind (live), transmission (new), pipelines, water, roads, rail
- Draw or upload the footprint → screen 11 hazards at sub-component level, with time and scenario deltas → AI-written report, "every number traceable back to source"
- "95% faster than manual"; replaces consultant desktop studies and black-box scores

## Who buys it
- Infrastructure investors and funds, utilities, energy developers; investment committees and second-line risk

## Why it matters (my take)
- Their clearest example of AI writing the story while every number traces to source: the same pattern works for bank reporting
- Shows expansion beyond buildings into infrastructure

## Opportunities (my take)
1. The traceable AI report for Spectra portfolio questions
2. Knock-on effects across a network (a substation failure hitting dependent assets)
3. Project finance view: covenant sensitivity to scenario changes
