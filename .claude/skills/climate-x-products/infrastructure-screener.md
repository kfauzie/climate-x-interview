
# Climate X Infrastructure Screener

Source tags: **[site]** climate-x.com (read 2026-11-01; Climate X's own claims), **[analysis]** my inference. Present [analysis] items as hypotheses, never as facts.

## What it is [site]
- An app for **spatially complex infrastructure** that "doesn't fit on a single point on a map": solar and wind (live), energy transmission (new), pipelines, water utilities, roads, rail
- **Three steps:** (1) define the footprint: upload assets, use an existing footprint or draw boundaries (point, line, polygon or network); (2) screen 11 hazards: exposure, elevated-risk zones, time-horizon and scenario deltas at sub-component level; (3) **AI-enhanced narrative report**, "IC-ready… every number traceable back to source"
- Claims "95% faster than manual"; replaces "weeks of consultant-led desktop studies", "fragmented data across hazard sources" and "black-box scores"
- Customer quote (Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners): "the progression from exposure to vulnerability, to impact assessment, and ultimately financial materiality"

## Customers and users
- Infrastructure investors and funds, utilities, energy developers; investment committees and second-line risk [site]

## Why it matters [analysis]
- It's Climate X's clearest public example of the **AI-plus-traceability pattern**: AI writes the narrative, every number traces to source. The same pattern applies to bank reporting (answers with their method)
- Shows expansion beyond buildings into infrastructure, a fast-growing segment (data centres and renewables are named in prep notes)
