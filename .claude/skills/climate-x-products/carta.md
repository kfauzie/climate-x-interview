# Carta

## What it is
- Asset maps for 18.7m public and private companies: sites and subsidiaries (incl. JVs >50%)
- Pipeline: websites, job boards, registries, maps → geolocated, classified, scored for materiality → AI + LLMs + human review of every output → satellite checks on key sites → into Spectra or the client's systems
- Annual remapping, anomaly detection, spot checks
- Managed service; one counterparty or hundreds; "days, not months"
- UI: company search, map, asset directory (parent, subsidiary, premise type, criticality, address), confidence split (e.g. 90% high), CSV export, "Spectra →"
- Claims: beats standalone LLMs; used by a top-3 investment bank; CBRE

## Who buys it
- Banks (corporate borrowers, provisions), investment banks, PE and asset managers (due diligence), insurers, advisers

## Gaps (my take)
- No complete golden set: accuracy hard to prove
- Missed sites understate risk; wrong sites cost trust
- Thin footprints for small and private firms
- Managed service scales with people; annual remap can lag deals

## Opportunities (my take)
1. Provenance per site: sources, checks, confidence, so an analyst can defend it
2. Evaluation without a full golden set: validate on companies with known site lists; track precision and recall; use corrections
3. Self-serve for well-known companies, managed service for complex ones
4. Customer corrections feed the models
5. Deal and credit pack: structure → assets → risk → adaptation options
6. Alerts when a counterparty opens, closes or sells key sites

## Questions
- How do you assess a borrower's physical risk today? How long does it take?
- Last time you relied on a company's asset list: how did you know it was complete?
- What would make you trust a site you hadn't seen before?
