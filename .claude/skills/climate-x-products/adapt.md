
# Climate X Adapt

Confidence tags: **[public]** from Climate X marketing or press (unverified), **[prep]** from my preparation notes, **[analysis]** my inference. Present [analysis] items as hypotheses, never as facts.

## What it is
- Models the cost and ROI of adaptation strategies such as building retrofits, flood mitigation and heat resilience [public]
- Helps decide which adaptation is worth investing in, based on the risks Spectra identifies [prep]
- Natural next step after Spectra: from "how much could we lose" to "what should we do about it, and is it worth it" [analysis]

## Customers and users
- **Real-estate owners and operators:** capex planning for resilience across a portfolio (e.g. Aroundtown) [public]
- **Facilities and services partners:** Mitie (UK Climate Change Risk Assessment service, launched June 2026), Equans, IBD Group (resilience plans and disclosures) [public]
- **Banks:** Triodos uses Spectra and Adapt in SME lending risk management [public]
- Likely users: asset managers, sustainability leads, facilities and capex planners; consultants working on their behalf [analysis]

## Value proposition
- Turns risk into a **prioritised, costed action plan** with a financial case: spend £X to avoid £Y of expected loss
- Commercial framing: protecting asset value, rental income, insurability and access to finance, not safety alone [prep steer]

## Strengths [analysis]
- Builds on Spectra's loss model, so the "before vs after" loss is on the same basis
- Gives partners (consultants, facilities managers) something to sell: projects, not just reports. That makes Adapt a channel engine
- Moves Climate X from a cost centre (risk reporting) to value creation (investment decisions)

## Weaknesses and risks [analysis]
- **Cost and effectiveness assumptions** for adaptations vary by site and contractor. Customers will ask "how do you know this barrier cuts loss by 40%?"
- ROI depends on horizon, discount rate and scenario, the same judgment-call problem as Spectra
- Benefit is avoided loss, which is hard to prove after the fact
- Buyer may differ from Spectra's (capex and operations vs risk), so a new sale and a new champion

## Opportunities (hypotheses to test) [analysis]
1. **Lending link:** feed adaptation into loan terms, e.g. resilience-linked or green loans where completed adaptation lowers the risk rating. Connects Adapt to bank revenue
2. **Portfolio capex optimiser:** given a budget, which assets and measures give the most avoided loss per £, with assumptions stated
3. **Transparent assumptions:** every measure's cost, effectiveness source and confidence visible and editable, so customers and partners can adjust to local quotes
4. **Partner workflow:** templates that turn Adapt output into a client proposal or a resilience plan for disclosures
5. **Track realised value:** record which measures were done and compare with later events, to build evidence for ROI claims

## Discovery questions (non-leading)
- After you see a high-risk asset, what happens next? Who decides whether to spend money on it?
- Tell me about the last adaptation or retrofit decision. What did the business case look like?
- How do you compare adaptation spend with other capex today?
- Who do you need to convince, and what evidence do they trust?
