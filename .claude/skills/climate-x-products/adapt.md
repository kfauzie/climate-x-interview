
# Climate X Adapt (and Adaptation Finance)

Source tags: **[site]** climate-x.com (read 2026-11-01; Climate X's own claims), **[web]** press or third parties, **[prep]** my preparation notes, **[analysis]** my inference. Present [analysis] items as hypotheses, never as facts.

## What it is [site]
- "Instant access to asset-level climate risk data and forecasts return on investment (ROI) for planning and implementing adaptation solutions." Upload an asset, get a report "in as little as 60 seconds"
- **The report (CSV):** pre-adaptation risk metrics, adaptation implementation costs, **1-year and 10-year ROI**, loss savings, and post-adaptation risk metrics. Example on the site: SSP2-4.5, flood defences on; building loss £33,828 before adaptation, £5,674 after, loss savings £28,154; hazard ratings drop (e.g. river flood E → A, storm D → B)
- **Standards:** ISO 14090 / 14091, UNFCCC, EU Taxonomy and technical guidance
- **Uses named on the site:** acquisitions and due diligence, valuation, finding undervalued assets, adaptation financing, insurance negotiations
- **Positioning:** replaces consultant-led adaptation assessments that "take weeks to mobilise" and slow or lose deals; "no 3rd party assessments"

## Adaptation Finance (offer for banks) [site]
- Pitch: "a $2 trillion climate resilience opportunity" (annual resilience funding need by 2026) that financing hasn't kept up with
- Process: (1) **generate leads**: the bank gives corporate client names, Climate X maps their assets (Carta) and finds the highest-ROI resilience projects; (2) **due diligence** by Climate X risk engineers, remote or on site; (3) **underwriting** support for front-line teams
- Value for banks: new revenue (fees and interest), stronger client relationships, lower credit risk. For corporates: protected asset value, continuity, steadier insurance costs
- Examples on the site: Hilton (143 assets mapped; $26.5m of financing with positive 10-year ROI; about $0.8m fee or interest income over 5 years); Hindalco (8,145 assets; $680m; $40m–$170m)

## Customers and users
- **Real-estate owners and operators** (Aroundtown, CBRE) [web, site]
- **Facilities and services partners:** Mitie (UK Climate Change Risk Assessment service, June 2026), Equans, IBD Group [web]
- **Banks:** Triodos uses Spectra and Adapt in SME lending risk management [web]; Adaptation Finance targets corporate and commercial banking teams [site]
- Likely users: asset managers, capex and facilities planners, deal teams, relationship managers, consultants [analysis]

## Value proposition
- Turns risk into a **costed, ranked action plan** with a financial case: spend £X to avoid £Y of expected loss, with ROI at 1 and 10 years [site]
- For banks it turns climate risk from a cost (reporting, provisions) into **revenue** (financing resilience) [site, analysis]

## Strengths [analysis]
- Same loss engine as Spectra, so before and after are on the same basis
- Gives partners and banks something to sell: projects and loans, not just reports
- Fast, self-serve report vs consultant timelines; standards-based

## Weaknesses and risks [analysis]
- **Cost and effectiveness assumptions** vary by site and contractor ("how do you know this defence cuts loss by 80%?"). Needs visible, editable assumptions
- ROI depends on horizon, discount rate and scenario: the same judgment-call problem as Spectra, and SSP5-8.5's retirement affects severe-case ROI
- Avoided loss is hard to prove after the fact
- Different buyer from Spectra's (capex, operations, relationship managers), so a new champion per sale

## Opportunities (hypotheses to test) [analysis]
1. **Lending link:** resilience-linked loan terms where completed adaptation lowers the risk rating; ties Adapt into bank revenue and risk appetite
2. **Portfolio capex optimiser:** given a budget, which assets and measures give the most avoided loss per £, with assumptions stated
3. **Transparent, editable assumptions:** cost, effectiveness source and confidence per measure, adjustable to local quotes
4. **Relationship-manager workflow:** turn a client's Adapt results into a financing proposal (the Adaptation Finance motion, productised)
5. **Track realised value:** record which measures were done and compare with later events, to build evidence for ROI claims

## Discovery questions (non-leading)
- After you see a high-risk asset, what happens next? Who decides whether to spend money on it?
- Tell me about the last adaptation or retrofit decision. What did the business case look like?
- How do you compare adaptation spend with other capex today?
- (Banks) When did climate resilience last come up in a conversation with a corporate client? What happened next?
- Who do you need to convince, and what evidence do they trust?
