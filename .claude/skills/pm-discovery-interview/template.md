# Discovery question template

Base version (from my Notion template, updated after mock runs). Copy per run; don't edit for one run.

### Opening
- Where is the need for X coming from? Tell me about the most recent requests
- Who would actually use X day to day? Would the people asking use it themselves?
- If not clear: Is anything making this more pressing right now?

### Walkthrough
- Walk me through the last time you did X. Each step: who, which tool, how long
  - Do these come up again and again, or are they mostly new?
  - Where does most of the time go?
  - What data goes in, and from where?
  - What's the most painful part? → What do you check, and why?
  - At what point does <the risk> start to change a decision for you? Why there?
- On a cue ("it queues on my desk"): "How's that been for you?"

### Value and trust
- Baseline: How much does this cost you today, in time or people? Besides time, what else does it cost you? When an answer was late or wrong, what did it hold up?
- Target: If this got better, how would you know?
- Guardrail: What would need to be true to trust <the answer> without <the painful step>?
- Sign-off: Who else signs it off, and who could block it? When someone last challenged a number, what did you show them?

### Close
- Recap, 3 points, biggest pain first. Anything I missed?
- I'll come back with something to review. Who else should be there?
- Can I email short questions in between?
- Could you share a few past requests and the answers your team gave? We'd check our answers against yours
