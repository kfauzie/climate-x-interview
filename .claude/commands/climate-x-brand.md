# Climate X Master Brand & Messaging Guide

This skill is the single source of truth for the Climate X corporate identity. It combines visual guidelines, brand voice, company facts, and market messaging.

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Voice

### 1.1 Personality
Climate X communicates **precision, resilience, urgency, and analytical depth**.
- **Intelligent and expert** — grounded in science and finance.
- **Clear and confident** — no jargon or waffle.
- **Bold and visionary** — resilience as a lever for growth.
- **Customer-empowering** — focused on their success.

### 1.2 TOV Dimensions
- **Action-Oriented:** "Turn resilience into returns."
- **Professional:** Audit-grade, regulatory-aligned.
- **Transparent:** No black-box scores.
- **Empowering:** Equip teams to lead.

### 1.3 What to Avoid
- **Vague ESG platitudes** ("sustainable future").
- **Alarmist framing** (doom and gloom).
- **Black-box language** (unexplained AI scores).
- **Advocacy/Activism language.**

## 2. Company Overview & Products

**Mission:** Transform climate science into financial clarity.
**Vision:** Make physical climate risk fundamental to all investment decisions.

### 2.1 The Product Suite
- **Carta (Asset Mapping):** Creates a trusted asset foundation; resolves location and attribute accuracy at scale.
- **Spectra (Physical Climate Risk):** Flagship platform. Projects scenarios to 2100 (CMIP6). Computes **AAL/EAL** across 11+ hazards.
- **Adapt (Resilience Planning):** Quantifies CapEx for adaptation and calculates expected ROI of resilience measures.
- **Business Disruption:** Quantifies indirect risk losses (productivity/asset downtime) from heat, flooding and wind.

### 2.2 Key Stats (Source of Truth)
- **AUM covered:** +$13.5tn
- **Assets mapped:** +2bn
- **Global GDP coverage:** +93%
- **Hazard coverage:** 11+ hazards (flooding, heat, storms, etc.)
- **Regulatory alignment:** CSRD, TCFD, SB 261, SEC.

## 3. Personas & Market Messaging

### 3.1 ICP & Personas
- **The Risk Analyst:** Wants transparent, auditable, non-black-box data. Trigger: "No black-box scores."
- **The Head of Sustainability:** Wants credibility and speed to compliance. Trigger: "Equip your team to lead."
- **The Portfolio Manager:** Wants to price risk into valuations faster. Trigger: "Price physical risk into investments."
- **The CRO:** Wants proven methodology and IT/Model Risk compliance. Trigger: "Audit-grade insights."

### 3.2 Sector Specifics
- **Asset Management:** Use **AAL** (Average Annual Loss). Focus: ROI, due diligence, and competitive advantage.
- **Banking:** Use **EAL** (Expected Annual Loss). Focus: Precision in credit risk frameworks and regulatory stress testing.

### 3.3 Core Pain Points & "Yes" Triggers
- **Pain Points:** Data gaps, model opacity (black boxes), regulatory complexity, resource constraints, vendor fragmentation.
- **What Makes Them Say YES:** Clear transparency (no black box), peer validation (social proof), speed to value (minutes, not months), end-to-end coverage, and quantified ROI (AAL/avoided losses).

### 3.4 Regional Nuances
- **UK:** TCFD mandatory; focus on regulatory alignment.
- **EU:** CSRD driving fast adoption; focus on multi-hazard coverage.
- **US:** ROI and competitive advantage angle works better than compliance.

## 4. Visual Identity (Quick Ref)

### 4.1 Logo & The X
- The **"X"** is the central symbol of the unknown variable we decipher.
- Clear space: **0.5X** factor.
- Primary logo: "CLIMATE" (grey), "X" (red/grey).

### 4.2 Colors
- **Red (dominant):** Tech Red (#e72d41), Red X (#e52321).
- **Greys (analytical):** X Grey 6 (#343a40 - primary text), X Grey 7 (#212529).
- **Accent:** Jade Green (#2bad72) — use sparingly.

### 4.3 Typography
- **Graphik:** Primary brand font for all headers and body.
- **Arial:** For email and when Graphik is unavailable (size 11, #343a40).

## 5. Copy Checklist
- [ ] Leads with a financial/business outcome?
- [ ] Uses active verbs (quantify, price, unlock, protect)?
- [ ] Specific data points/hazards included?
- [ ] Correct hazard/loss terminology (AAL vs EAL)?
- [ ] No "black box" or "ESG waffle" language?
- [ ] Logo and colors match background?

## 6. Applying it to product screens
- **Colour roles:** Tech Red for the main action and the X; X Grey 6 for text, X Grey 7 for headings; off-white #f9f9fa background, white panels; Jade Green only for positive or done states
- **Font:** Graphik; Arial where Graphik isn't available (e.g. prototypes)
- **Grades and scores always say what they mean** in £ or plain words (no black-box scores)
- **Loss term:** AAL for asset management, private equity and real estate investors; EAL for banking
