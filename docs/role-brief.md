# Role brief: Principal Product Manager (AI-native B2B), Climate X

Sources: the recruiter's role description and interview prep pages, plus my first-round notes (Notion, Q4 2026 goals → Interview prep → ClimateX). Confidential, shared under NDA.

## The role in one line
A senior hands-on IC seat: own a core product surface end to end, from customer discovery to shipped, validated, revenue-generating product, in a two-person pod (1 PM + 1 engineer), shipping monthly.

## What it is
- **Senior IC, not a management track.** A leader's remit and influence without the org chart. Paid and promoted for building
- **Forward-deployed.** Sit with customers, find the problem first-hand, prove value in weeks rather than quarters
- **Builder, not spec-writer.** Use AI natively to design, prototype and ship, and take your own work as far as a pull request. Engineers own the infrastructure; the PM owns the customer understanding and the product shape
- **Broad remit:** can run more than one build at once, across several teams
- **Sets the standard** for how the team uses AI to design and ship
- **Brings enterprise stakeholders through fast change** without needing air cover
- Explicitly *not* an infrastructure or platform PM role

## Non-negotiables
- **Startup or scale-up building experience.** Enterprise-only backgrounds are ruled out, however senior
- **Evidence of zero-to-one → production → revenue.** "Evidence not narrative"
- Wants to stay a hands-on IC
- B2B preferred. Exceptional B2C builders are considered only if they show enterprise-grade thinking

## The two seats (they are hiring both)
| | Seat 1: Customer & UX / Discovery | Seat 2: Agentic AI / LLM |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Lives in the customer's problem, constant discovery, strong UX | Builds model-driven products and owns how they behave in production |
| Bar | Takes own work to a PR an engineer can merge with few changes | Explains the eval setup in detail: golden datasets, human in the loop, thresholds, feedback loops |
| Proof | Zero-to-one into production and revenue | Shipped agentic or LLM products to real users, plus the same revenue track record |

## Working model
- Flat, lean, AI-native, high trust. Decisions are made in the room
- 3 days a week in the London office
- Comp: base to £150k+, with room to stretch, plus options and benefits

## Process
1. 45 min with Kevin Luo (VP / head of product). ✅ Done
2. **90 min live working session.** Kevin role-plays an enterprise customer and hands over real Climate X data to build on. Assessed on reasoning and outcome, not volume
3. Kamil Kluza (co-founder) if it goes well. ⚠️ The role page puts the co-founder inside the live session; the candidate's latest information is that the Kamil conversation follows only if it goes well
4. Final conversation with Lukky (CEO)

- Can I get from Kevin's stated ask to the real workflow problem before building? (Seat 1)

- Do I reach a PR, not a spec?
