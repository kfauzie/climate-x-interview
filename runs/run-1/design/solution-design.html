Re: How much expected losses rise from 2030 to 2050, severe scenario
Hi,

Under the severe scenario, our expected climate losses rise 2.81× from 2030 to 2050: from £1.7m a year to £4.7m a year.

For comparison: 1.58× under the central scenario (today's policies) and 1.19× with emissions cut fast. Most of the rise comes from subsidence.

How it was calculated, and the loans, are in the attached file.
