# Run 1: discovery questions (Sarah Okafor, Head of Climate Risk)

**What she asked for (X):** "a customised report builder to help her team produce custom reports to answer any questions that the team may have."

## Questions
1. **The ask.** Where is the need for a report builder coming from? Tell me about the most recent request your team had to answer.
2. **The last example.** Walk me through the last time: who asked, who did what, in which tool, and how long it took. Would the people asking use a report builder themselves?
   - Which climate scenario and year did you use for it, and who decided that? *(The scenario alone changes the total 2.6×, so the answer decides whether the product pre-sets a standard or asks each time.)*
3. **Where the time goes.** Where did most of the time go, and what made it painful?
4. **The value.** If this got better, how would it benefit you: in time, money, or better decisions?
5. **Trust and success.** How would you know it's working? What would you need to see to trust a number from it, and who signs it off?
6. **Close.** Recap in three points, biggest problem first. Anything I missed? Can I email you short questions?
