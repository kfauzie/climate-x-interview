# Run 1: discovery questions for Sarah Okafor (Head of Climate Risk)

Customised from the `discovery-interview` skill's template, using this run's `RUN.md` and the Profile findings in `notebooks/spectra_profile.ipynb`.
**X** = turning the quarterly Spectra delivery into the physical-risk numbers her audiences ask for. If the session opens with a specific ask, replace X with that ask.
**Time budget: 15 minutes, 7 questions.** Use sub-probes only if the answer is vague. `(from: …)` tags are for me only. Never read them out.

## Live (15 min)

### 1. Opening (2 min)
*Expect: regulatory and committee reporting (PRA / ICAAP); pricing and credit are newer asks.*
- **Q1.** Where is the need for this coming from right now? Who asked most recently, and who uses the numbers once your team has produced them?

### 2. Workflow (8 min)
*Expect: most time goes on analysts reshaping the export and reconciling numbers between audiences.*
- **Q2.** Walk me through the last time a quarterly Spectra delivery arrived, from the file landing to a number in a deck.
  - Probes: who does each step, in which tool, and how long? Where does most of the time go? What data goes in besides Spectra, and from where? What's the most painful part?
- **Q3.** At what point does a physical risk start to change a decision for you, in credit, pricing or risk appetite? Why there? Is it the same for mortgages and CRE?
- **Q4.** When two people report a portfolio climate number, how do you make sure they match? Tell me about a time they didn't. (from: scenario, year and hazard choices swing totals by large multiples)

### 3. Value and trust (3 min)
*Expect: analyst days per quarter, plus credibility risk in front of the PRA.*
- **Q5.** What does a quarterly cycle cost you today: analyst days, rework, consultants, delays? Has a climate number ever changed, or failed to change, a lending decision?
- **Q6.** If you got the number without your team's manual steps, what would you need to see to trust it, and defend it to the PRA? Who else has to sign it off?

### 4. Close (2 min)
- **Q7.** Recap, biggest problem first: "So the biggest issue is…, then…" Anything I missed?
  - Next step: a prototype review with you, one of your analysts, and someone from pricing or credit. Who else?
  - Could I email you or your analysts short questions in between?
  - Could you share a past committee or PRA pack, the analysts' spreadsheet, or a case where a number was challenged, so we can test against real answers?

### Follow-up triggers (any time)
- "usually", "it depends" → "Tell me about the last time"
- "roughly", "should be fine" → "What makes it roughly?"
- A simple step (e.g. "we just pivot it") taking days → "Walk me through that step"
