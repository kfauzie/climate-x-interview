# Run 1: discovery questions for Sarah Okafor (Head of Climate Risk)

Customised from the `discovery-interview` skill's template, using this run's `RUN.md` and the Profile findings in `notebooks/spectra_profile.ipynb`.
**X** = turning the quarterly Spectra delivery into the physical-risk numbers her audiences ask for. If the session opens with a specific ask, replace X with that ask.
★ = must-ask (about 7). `(from: …)` tags are for me only. Never read them out or hint at the finding.

### Opening
*Hypothesis: the need comes from regulatory and committee reporting (PRA / ICAAP), with pricing and credit as newer asks.*
- ★ Could you tell me where the need for this is coming from right now? Who asked most recently?
  - If not obvious: who actually uses the numbers once your team has produced them?
- Of the people who come to you (CRO, risk committee, credit committee, mortgage pricing, treasury, sustainability, IR, the PRA), whose questions take the most work to answer?

### Detailed walkthrough
*Hypothesis: most of the time goes into analysts reshaping the export and reconciling numbers between audiences, not into analysis.*
- ★ Could you walk me through the last time a new quarterly Spectra delivery arrived? From the file landing to a number in a deck
  - Who on your team does what, in which tool, and how long does each step take?
  - Where does most of the time go?
  - What data goes in besides Spectra (loan book, valuations, LTV, insurance, postcodes), and where does each come from?
  - What makes it painful?
- ★ At what point does a physical risk start to change a decision for you, in credit, pricing or risk appetite? Why at that point? Is it the same for mortgages and CRE?
- Data-informed:
  - ★ When two people report a portfolio climate number, how do you make sure they match? Tell me about a time they didn't (from: scenario, year and hazard choices swing totals by large multiples)
  - Which scenario and time horizon do you report, and who decided? Has that been challenged? (from: SSP585 vs SSP245, 2030 vs 2050 swings)
  - How do you count a property that secures more than one loan? (from: 112 shared properties, 232 loans)
  - What happens when a loan comes back without a score for a hazard? Who notices? (from: 45 loans missing a hazard × year)
  - How do you get from damage to the property to an impact on the loan, for ICAAP or credit? (from: property loss vs LTV-weighted loss)
  - How do you look at your largest CRE exposures compared with the mortgage book? (from: concentration; CRE is about 73% of loss)
  - Have you joined this data to anything else, like valuations, EPC or insurance? How did that go? (from: 159 malformed postcodes)
  - How do you compare this quarter with last quarter? What happens when a number moves? (from: quarterly cadence for about 18 months)
- Follow up if hearing:
  - "usually", "it depends" → "Tell me about the last time"
  - "roughly", "should be fine" → "What makes it roughly?"
  - a simple step (e.g. "we just pivot it") taking days → "Walk me through that step"

### Metrics & approval
*Hypothesis: the cost is analyst days per quarter plus credibility risk in front of the PRA.*
- ★ **Baseline:** How much does a quarterly cycle cost you today: analyst days, rework, consultants, or delays to committee?
- **Baseline, commercial:** Has a climate number ever changed, or failed to change, a lending or pricing decision? What did that cost?
- **Target:** If this got easier, how would you know it's working? What would you measure?
- ★ **Guardrail:** If you got the number without your team's manual steps, what would you need to see to trust it and defend it to the PRA?
- **Process:** Besides you, who has to sign it off? Who could block a new way of producing these numbers (model risk, internal audit, IT)?

### Closing
- ★ Recap, biggest problem first: "So the biggest issue is…, then…, then…" Anything I missed?
- Next step: I'll come back with a prototype to review. Who else should be in that review: one of your analysts, and someone from pricing or credit?
- Could I email you or your analysts short questions in between?
- For testing: could you share a past committee or PRA pack, the spreadsheet your analysts use, or a case where a number was challenged? It would help us check outputs against real answers
