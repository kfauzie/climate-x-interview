# Run 1: discovery questions (Sarah Okafor, Head of Climate Risk)

**What she asked for (X):** "a customised report builder to help her team produce custom reports to answer any questions that the team may have." Find the problem behind it before discussing any builder.

**10 minutes.** Six core questions, then up to three data questions if there's time.

## Core questions
1. **The ask.** Where is the need for a report builder coming from? Tell me about the most recent request your team had to answer.
2. **The last example.** Walk me through the last time: who asked, who did what, in which tool, and how long it took. Would the people asking use a report builder themselves?
3. **Where the time goes.** Where did most of the time go, and what made it painful?
4. **The value.** If this got better, how would it benefit you: in time, money, or better decisions?
5. **Trust and success.** How would you know it's working? What would you need to see to trust a number from it, and who signs it off?
6. **Close.** Recap in three points, biggest problem first. Anything I missed? Can I email you short questions?

## From the data (only if there's time)
| Ask | Why (from the data) | How the answer changes the product |
|---|---|---|
| When someone asks you for a climate number, which scenario and year do you use? Who decided that? | The scenario alone changes the total 2.6× (£2.4m low to £6.1m very high, 2050) | A fixed standard → the product pre-sets it and flags changes. Case by case → it asks, and shows each option |
| Where in the book does the climate risk sit, as you see it? Does that change how you report or act? | One retail property in the South East is 12.8% of expected loss; the top 1% of properties are 32% | If it's concentrated, the product shows the loans behind every number, not just totals |
| Do you ever get asked about loss to the bank, or how risk changed since last quarter? How do you answer that today? | The file only has damage to properties, from one quarter | If yes, the product must say plainly it can't answer those yet, or more data comes first |
