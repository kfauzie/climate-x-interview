# Run 1: discovery questions (Sarah Okafor, Head of Climate Risk)

**What she asked for (X):** "a customised report builder to help her team produce custom reports to answer any questions that the team may have."

## Questions
1. **The ask.** Where is the need for a report builder coming from?
2. **The last example.** Walk me through the last time: who asked, who did what, in which tool, and how long it took.
   - Would the people asking use a report builder themselves?
   - Which climate scenario and year did you use for it? *(The scenario alone changes the total 2.6×, so this decides whether the product pre-sets a standard or asks each time.)*
   - Who decided that?
   - *\<If they name a choice or step you didn't expect, ask how it was decided last time\>*
3. **Pain points.** Where did most of the time go?
   - What made it painful?
   - *\<Push when it's vague, hedged or suspiciously slow: "Why does that take two days? It seems simple."\>*
   - *\<If they admit a weakness ("it's not perfectly consistent"), ask "What happens when it isn't?"\>*
4. **The value.** If this got better, how would it benefit you: in time, money, or better decisions?
   - How would you know it's working?
5. **Trust.** What would you need to see to trust a number from it?
   - *\<If they've already covered it, shorten: "You mentioned X. Anything else?"\>*
   - Who signs it off?
6. **Close.** **\<Recap\>** Play it back in three points, biggest problem first, in their words and with their numbers. Anything I missed?
   - Can I email you short questions?
