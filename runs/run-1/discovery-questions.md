# Run 1: discovery questions for Sarah Okafor (Head of Climate Risk)

Customised from the `pm-2-discovery-interview` skill's template, using this run's `RUN.md` and the Profile findings in `notebooks/spectra_profile.ipynb`.
**Ask (her words):** a customised report builder to help her team produce custom reports to answer any questions the team may have.
**X** = producing a custom report to answer a question about physical risk in the book. The ask is a solution: find the questions and the pain behind it before discussing any builder.
**Time budget: 15 minutes, 7 questions.** Use sub-probes only if the answer is vague. `(from: …)` tags are for me only. Never read them out.

## Live (15 min)

### 1. Opening (2 min)
*Expect: ad-hoc questions from committees, pricing and the PRA that the quarterly output doesn't answer directly.*
- **Q1.** Where is the need for a report builder coming from? Tell me about the most recent question your team couldn't answer quickly. Who asked it, and why?

### 2. Workflow (8 min)
*Expect: analysts re-cut the quarterly export by hand in Excel for each request; most time goes on reshaping and checking, not analysis.*
- **Q2.** Walk me through how your team answered that question, from the request coming in to the report going out.
  - Probes: who did each step, in which tool, and how long? Where did most of the time go? What data went in besides Spectra, and from where? What was the most painful part?
- **Q3.** What kinds of questions come in, from whom, and how often? Which repeat every quarter, and which are genuinely new? Which actually change a decision in credit, pricing or risk appetite?
- **Q4.** When two people on your team answer the same question, how do you make sure the numbers match? Tell me about a time they didn't. (from: scenario, year and hazard choices swing totals by large multiples)

### 3. Value and trust (3 min)
*Expect: days per custom request, several requests a quarter, plus credibility risk in front of the CRO or the PRA.*
- **Q5.** How long does a custom request take today, and how many do you get a quarter? Has a slow or inconsistent answer ever affected a decision?
- **Q6.** If your analysts could get an answer without the manual steps, what would you need to see to trust it and put it in front of the CRO or the PRA? Who else has to sign it off?

### 4. Close (2 min)
- **Q7.** Recap, biggest problem first: "So the biggest issue is…, then…" Anything I missed?
  - Next step: a prototype review with you and one of your analysts. Who else should see it: pricing, credit, model risk?
  - Could I email you or your analysts short questions in between?
  - Could you share a few recent questions with the reports your team produced, and the analysts' spreadsheet? We'd use them to test answers against yours

### Follow-up triggers (any time)
- "any question", "it depends" → "Tell me about the last three questions you got"
- "usually", "roughly", "should be fine" → "Tell me about the last time" / "What makes it roughly?"
- A simple step (e.g. "we just filter it") taking days → "Walk me through that step"
- A feature request ("a dropdown for…") → "What would that let you do that you can't today?"
