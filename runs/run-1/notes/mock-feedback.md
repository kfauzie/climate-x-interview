# Run 1: mock interview feedback (from the role-play, out of character)

Run 1 specific. General lessons from this were folded into the `discovery-interview` skill.

## What I uncovered
- **Volume:** asking for the most recent questions got about fifteen a month straight away
- **Repeats:** "Do they repeat, or are they mostly new?" got that only the risk committee pack repeats
- **Where the time goes:** the walkthrough (each step, tool and timing) got the deciding and defending
- **Bottleneck:** "What's the most painful part?" → "What do you check?" got her review queue
- **Trust:** "What would need to be true to trust it?" got model risk and traceable numbers
- **Who asks (half):** senior people ask in plain English, but I never asked whether they'd use a report builder. That's the insight that kills the ask. "Who would actually use it?" earns it
- **Follow-ups:** only via "anything I missed?". Fine, it did its job

## What I missed
- **How it lands on her:** "What does that mean for you and your team?" after "it queues on my desk" would have got her anxiety and an analyst thinking of leaving
- **Consequences beyond time:** "Besides time, what else does it cost you?" would have got climate risk being seen as a blocker, and the risk of a wrong figure reaching the PRA

## Slips
- "Roughly, yes": I labelled it "deciding the scenarios" and moved on; the review part only came out later
- Asked for the volume twice
- Jargon: "model training and eval"
- Recap was long and led with volume. It should have led with her being the bottleneck

## Next time
When I hear "roughly", ask "What's the part I'm missing?"
