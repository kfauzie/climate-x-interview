## The firm

CX Capital is a mid-market private equity firm headquartered in London, investing across Europe with selective global exposure. £6bn AUM across three funds, twenty-four portfolio companies. Equity cheques of £40m–£120m, hold periods of four to seven years.

Climate X onboarded CX Capital in Q1 2025. Carta for portfolio monitoring, with physical risk assessments run on request when a deal enters diligence. Renewal due Q1 2027.

## Who we work with

Sustainability sits centrally and covers all investment activity. Two people work on physical climate risk across forty to sixty deals a year, reporting to a Managing Director who sponsors the work but has not made it mandatory.

The function has no formal role in diligence and no authority over deal teams. Participation is at each deal team's discretion. Assessments were requested on eleven of sixty deals last year.

## How diligence works at CX Capital

Four to six weeks from kick-off to Investment Committee. The IC paper is drafted from week four. Fifteen or more workstreams run in parallel, each with an owner and a RAG status. The IC paper template contains a standing ESG section.

## Observations from the account team

- Assessments are requested inconsistently and usually late.
- The sustainability team has asked more than once whether the output can be made "more useful to the deal side." Unresolved.
- A deal exec asked what a letter grade meant in practice. The team couldn't answer it from the export.
- Hold period is not in what we send, and sits with the deal team.
- No deal has been declined on physical climate grounds. Where findings are acted on, it's through the post-close 100-day plan.
